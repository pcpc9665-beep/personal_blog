## Post-Internship Journey

However, I didn't delve deeply into the concepts during my internship, and a few months passed that way. Meanwhile, I applied for other interviews. I received a call, but the process didn't move forward because my communication skills were poor. That was when I realized I lacked the necessary communication skills. I tried to improve them, but due to laziness, I didn't do much or put in enough effort.

Since the projects I handled during my internship were minor, I started a new project to boost my skills. I began it last April, and even now—months later—it remains not completed. During this period, there were significant innovations in the AI sector. The demand shifted from developers who simply write standard code to freshers who know how to analyze code generated by AI.

I spoke with a company CEO who was willing to hire me; however, since their hiring is routed through Wipro, he advised me to acquire a few more skills first. I then added the following skills to my resume:

- **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript (ES6+), React.js, Next.js, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS, Redux
- **Backend**: Node.js, Express.js, REST APIs, Prisma ORM, Auth.js (NextAuth)
- **Databases**: PostgreSQL, MongoDB
- **AI & Automation**: RAG, n8n Workflow Automation, Prompt Engineering
- **DevOps & Deployment**: Git, GitHub Actions, Docker, Vercel
- **Testing & Tools**: Playwright (E2E), Jest, Postman
- **Integrations**: Stripe API, Cloudinary

I sent him my resume on June 20, 2026, and he asked me to call him one day. When I did, he told me that instead of hiring, the company was currently laying people off. Hearing that was disheartening, but I continued with my studies.

In the meantime, I used to work part-time in photography with my elder brother. Sometimes, I would be away from home for two or three days, making it impossible to study during that time. Days would pass like that—sometimes due to a lack of motivation to study, and other times because I was busy with work.

It has been quite a while since I finished my internship (from December 2025 to the present). I have applied for numerous jobs but haven't received a single call back. I am currently trying to learn the technologies required for the industry, though I am unsure where to start, how to begin, or what to focus on. Right now, I am studying system design.

That is my current situation.
